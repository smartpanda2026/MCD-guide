💡 Key facts

  • 🔹 MCD = ~10–17% adult nephrotic syndrome
  • 🔹 Children >90% of nephrotic cases
  • 🔹 Steroid response 80–95% adults
  • 🔹 Relapse ~50% → steroid-sparing agents
  • 🔹 Anti-nephrin in >40% primary MCD
  • 🔹 Rituximab: 2025 JAMA RCT — 87.4% vs 38.0% relapse-free at 49 weeks
  • 🔹 No head-to-head RCTs between rituximab, cyclophosphamide, and CNIs in adult FR/SD MCD
